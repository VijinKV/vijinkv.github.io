Ball Party – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026  ·  App: Ball Party (formerly Neon Dodge, com.vijin.neondodge) on Google Play  ·  Developer: ShapeCubes (Vijin Vincent)

In short: Ball Party does not ask for, collect, store or sell any personal information itself. There are no accounts or sign-ins. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data (such as the advertising ID and IP address) to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information the app stores on your device

To remember your progress and preferences, Ball Party saves the following only on your device (in the app's local storage). It is never sent to the developer or anyone else:

  • Your best score
  • Your sound (mute) and vibration on/off settings
  • Your coin balance, unlocked ship skins, equipped skin, daily-reward streak and claim date, and daily mission progress (all gameplay meta saved only on-device)

This data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ball Party may show an occasional full-screen interstitial between runs at natural breaks (never during active gameplay), and offers optional rewarded ads (for example "Watch ad to continue" or "Double coins"). There are no banner ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may automatically collect and share with Google:

DataPurpose
IP address (may be used to estimate your general location)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (e.g. ad views, taps, app launches)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (e.g. app/SDK performance, launch time, hang rate, energy usage)Analytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). The developer does not receive, see or control this data beyond aggregated ad reports provided by AdMob. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regulated regions): where required by law, the app shows Google's consent message on first launch so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. If a Privacy options link is shown on the main menu, you can change your choice at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Play offline: the game works without an internet connection; in that case no ads are loaded.

4. Permissions used

Ball Party does not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or accounts.

5. Children

Ball Party is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help address it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device advertising settings.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
